<p>We are continuing our advocacy work and are lobbying decision makers to add a multimodal pathway to the Alto project and make sure that local benefits are added to the Alto project mandate. Our advocacy work is expected to continue as the Alto project progresses.</p>

On June 22, 2026 Alto released its [What We Heard Report](https://www.altotrain.ca/en/public-consultation). This Public Consultation Report is Alto's summary of feedback gathered during the first phase of their public consultation process, as well as through Indigenous consultation activities that were conducted from October 2025 through June 2026 across the Toronto–Québec City Corridor.

While the What We Heard report acknowledges many of the stakeholder concerns, it does so in a fragmented manner which systematically minimizes their commonality. The report fails to treat community severance, trail continuity, municipal costs, the non-station community benefit gap and a parallel multimodal pathway as interconnected corridor-wide policy issues. In our view these are all "Local Benefits" issues that must be addressed in a cohesive way. 

The What We Heard report states that the Project Outcomes are:
1. Significantly increase intercity rail passengers
2. Enable safe intercity journeys
3. Enhance passenger experience in the corridor
4. Minimize financial costs to taxpayers
5. Support the Goevernment of Canada's net-zero commitment
6. Contribute positively to the Government of Canada's commitment to reconciliation with Indigenous Peoples
7. Significantly increase the availability of accessible and affordable services

We suggest that another project outcome must be:
8. Provide local benefit along the corridor

If local benefits are not explicitly part of the project outcomes then they will continue to be minimized and treated as after-thoughts. Local benefits to communities along the corridor must be part of the Alto project mandate.

You can see the timelines of the Alto project itself at the [Alto website](https://www.altotrain.ca/en/about-alto/whats-happening)
